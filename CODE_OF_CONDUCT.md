# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a respectful, constructive community where people with different backgrounds and experience can collaborate safely. Treat others with patience and respect; focus criticism on ideas and code, not people; and accept good-faith corrections.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and deliberately disrupting discussions or project spaces.

## Scope

This applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, community channels, and other spaces when someone is representing the agentRamen project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository owner's GitHub profile. For security vulnerabilities, use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/palrajjp/agentRamen/security/advisories/new) instead.

Maintainers will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality where possible, and take proportionate action. Actions may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who are the subject of a report should not participate in deciding its outcome.
